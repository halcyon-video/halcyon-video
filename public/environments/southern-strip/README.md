# Southern commercial strip

Original generated artwork, approved by the owner for Halcyon's fictional 1990s American retail setting. This is a fictional Southern commercial strip, not a photograph or reconstruction of a real address. It contains no intentionally reproduced real-chain marks.

Four lighting variants derive from one base layout: morning, afternoon, sunset and night. The native 1774 × 887 sources are retained without claiming added detail from upscaling. They are display-referred images, not measured HDR lighting maps.

The established Three.js GroundedSkybox supplies the distant scenery. The level ground is geometry in every view: the parking lot, the through street, their walks, curbs, verges and stall markings, and a matched pavement surround that carries the ground out toward the horizon and fades into the photograph there. The pavement samples the photograph's ground color for every time of day, and the walks, curbs and verges beyond the building's own walk follow that sample, so they keep the photograph's light rather than the scene's. The photograph supplies the neighboring buildings, their lots and distant vehicles. One orientation and location are retained across quality tiers and time changes.

Provenance: original image-generation work for Halcyon, October 2026. Higher-resolution source detail and the panorama seam can be improved in a future asset revision without changing the store layout or projection pipeline.
