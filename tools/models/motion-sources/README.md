The four BVH files are unmodified source captures from Female 1. The JSON files
are decoded samples generated by Three.js BVHLoader, not new authored motion.
`node tools/models/sample-clerk-captures.mjs` regenerates them.

Adaptations in `clerk-captured-motion.py`: standing calibration to the destination
rig, removal of horizontal travel for in-place sprite playback, crop selection,
a small distributed quaternion correction to join loop endpoints, relaxed finger
poses (source has no articulated finger motion), and hand clearance through the
destination standing calibration. The exporter grounds the deformed shoe soles.
The original motion timing and joint motion amplitudes are retained.

The walking portions precede the stopping portions of the first two takes.
Clip labels describe these review candidates, not a claim that one is the only
feminine or natural way to walk. The owner's earlier heel-past-chest constraint
was superseded by the request for natural captured strides on 2026-10-04.
