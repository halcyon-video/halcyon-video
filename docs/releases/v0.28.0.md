# Halcyon Video 0.28.0

A small release about the people who drive to the store and the signs they read once they are inside.

- Customers now arrive in one of four original, unbadged late-1980s body styles: a sedan, a hatchback, a station wagon and a minivan. Each has shaped panels, real wheel arches, tinted glass, wraparound bumpers, lamps, mirrors and dished wheel covers.
- Every regular owns one car and keeps the same body style and paint on every visit, so a car in the lot tells you who is inside. Paint comes from a twelve-colour period palette, chosen separately from the body style, and a store set before a car's model year never shows that car.
- The actor and studio spotlight stands feature a new subject every week instead of always the same one, and the two stands of a kind never show the same subject.
- A featured film with no title art now has its title lettered across the backdrop of its stand header, breaking long titles onto two lines.
- The VIDEO GAMES banners keep their lettering at the banner's own proportions instead of stretching it.
- JOIN TODAY membership cards now hang on just two aisle ends per store.
- When your RomM server reports logos for its platforms, the games department can hang a plaque with each platform's logo over its bays in place of the general banner. Logos load from your server while the app runs, and a logo that fails to load leaves the platform's name lettered on its plaque.

Low and medium quality load a lighter version of each car, and cars of the same colour share one paint material.
