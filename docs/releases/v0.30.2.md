# Halcyon Video 0.30.2

A small release: hooking up an EmulationStation game folder now takes one step, and its covers reach the shelves quickly.

- The store's power menu has an EmulationStation button. Pick your game folder once, and the games department and its consoles switch on and stock without reloading the page.
- Game covers from a picked folder load as the shelves are stocked, starting with the ones nearest to you, so the boxes show their art in seconds rather than a minute or more.
- The Steam companion lets Steam refresh an aged sign-in before treating it as expired, so library refreshes keep working.
- The morning sky outside the store no longer shows a pale rectangle at its upper left.
