# Halcyon Video 0.27.0

The Halloween release. Through October the store dresses for the season, and on November 1 it goes back to normal on its own.

- Arched black-cat clings join the Halloween window clings, which now continue along the side-wall windows as well as the storefront.
- The counter pumpkin hops and rocks when you bump the counter beside it. From October 16 it is carved as a candle-lit jack-o'-lantern.
- A black garland swags along the counter, taped at each point and strung with small shiny bats, pumpkins and skulls.
- Every aisle end carries a paper cutout of a cat, pumpkin, ghost or bat standing above its top shelf.
- The Halloween stands now stock only October pictures, so Christmas titles stay off them.

Around the rest of the store, shoppers no longer wander behind the counter, the facade brick no longer shows through the top of the storefront glass, the ceiling uses one tile all the way to the windows, and the window backsplash keeps one thickness along the exit counter. The games bay's "what's NEW" card has moved off the games onto an end-cap blade. The 1990 store gets a refined column, counters and information sign, and its aisle-end bases stay in place through seasonal changes.

The app icon, browser icon, phone launcher icons and Android TV banner now wear the current blue Halcyon emblem. A gamepad no longer drives the store while its window is in the background, and returning to the window no longer fires a burst of button presses.

Every release now has its own page on the hosted changelog, styled like a mid-90s video-store home page, and Discord release posts link straight to it.

The October decorations build only in October and stay dormant the rest of the year. Repeated pieces are instanced, and the pumpkin animates only while it moves.
