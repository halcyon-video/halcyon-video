# Halcyon Video 0.29.0

The parking lot is rebuilt from the ground up, and the store floor gains new displays.

- The parking lot is now fully modeled instead of borrowing the backdrop photograph's blurry ground. Asphalt stays sharp at every distance, stall lines are always painted, every car sits in a stall, and curbed grass strips, walks and a through street surround the lot. The pavement runs well out before it fades into the backdrop.
- At night on phones, the walks around the lot no longer glow near-white.
- The inner aisle ends in the 1990 store now stand on projecting plinths like the outer ones.
- A promotion for a single film gets its own corrugated floor display.
- Actor spotlights stand in a lamp-lit column topped with a marquee.
- In the 2000 and 2010 stores, the checkout sits under an angular bulkhead over a gray tiled entrance, with a pale band above the glass and silver window and door frames.
- Entering the store on a phone is quicker: fixture and ceiling shaders are prepared ahead of time, the counter loads first, and the 1990 clerk wears a lighter copy of the uniform.
