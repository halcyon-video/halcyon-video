# Halcyon Video v0.16.0

**[Browse all 44 screenshots in Discord](https://discord.com/channels/1539098045734584413/1546625970067349696)** · [Download the complete captioned gallery](https://github.com/halcyon-video/halcyon-video/releases/download/v0.16.0/halcyon-v0.16.0-screenshots.zip)

![Four views of the release](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/halcyon-video/halcyon-video/v0.16.0/docs/releases/v0.16.0.jpg)

The store has a new clerk, fitted display furniture, and a simpler way to change settings at the counter. This release also brings more greenery to the independent shop, steadier shelf browsing, and an automatic tour of the hosted store.

## A more finished sales floor

- Original Blender-authored shelf components add eased laminate edges, formed wire supports, finished end panels and fitted brackets. The freestanding shelf backs retain their pale finish.
- Genre endcaps, four-sided campaign stands and the bargain tub now use fitted modeled furniture while keeping their existing stock, signs and navigation.
- The counter terminal and keyboard have molded housings, keys, vents and curved display surfaces. The store's fixtures, clerk and ordinary surfaces respond to scene lighting.
- The clerk uses original Blender-rendered sprites, with idle, walking, stocking and talking poses and refined hands, cheeks and hair.
- The independent shop gains modeled ficus, pothos, palm and snake plants. Its libraries occupy adjacent shelf runs, and browsing follows the length of each row and back around.

## Settings belong at the counter

- Ordinary store settings stay on the counter's CRT. Staff options are part of the normal categories, and the terminal home has fewer branches.
- Store Theme is the single place to choose the independent shop. Media format, rental artwork and covers are grouped under Movie Cases.
- Store Brand and the Emblem Editor use the full screen when they need room. The live brand preview remains visible beside the scrolling choices, and the controls work with a remote or keyboard.
- Escaping boot returns to the store's setup terminal.

## Your colors reach the whole store

- Brand color edits repaint shelf trim, signs, endcaps, the counter and case emblems. Changing a brand no longer leaves cached case artwork behind.
- Carpet Colour can follow the era, the brand's primary color or its secondary color.
- Local brand drops handle filename casing and logo precedence more consistently.

## Shelf and counter details

- New Releases sections align with physical wall bays. A double feature uses two complete neighboring bays on the same wall, never a corner or a partial bay.
- The 2000 storefront's fascia sits flush against the front soffit. New Releases wall signage appears only in formats with that wall.
- The 2010 campaign adds lower-hanging rental cards over the media rows and a membership-services oval above the counter wing. The related window poster keeps its period gate, and bundled campaign art follows the active colors.
- Counter cards and props have clearer separation: the membership frame leaves the tip jar visible, the rental-policy frame stands on the counter band, and the tape-cleaner tray clears the rewind card. Acrylic cards have blank inner faces; the counter's mitered corner avoids a flickering stripe.

## Touring and rendering

- The hosted store can start a slow automatic camera tour after inactivity. Any real input ends the tour and returns control to the visitor.
- Boot starts cover downloads earlier, shares repeated image decodes and postpones lower-priority work. Resolution changes avoid unnecessary render-target resets, and ambient occlusion avoids an extra pass over transparent objects.

## Release verification

The production and hosted builds passed, together with all 498 tests. Both navigation roots passed their journey checks and 150-step seeded walks. Settings and editor checks passed at 1280×720, 1920×1080 and 1024×768.

Fresh local setup, the default streaming aisles, a single chosen service, and streaming link-out through the real controls passed. The built hosted version also passed three fresh boots, streaming link-out, four tour checkpoints and interruption by real input. A local runtime demo still accepts an available user-asset texture through the real override loader. This completes functional verification of the earlier boot work; no comparative loading-time claim is made.

These screenshots use the public Halcyon artwork in an isolated checkout without private user assets. Construction views are labeled separately from in-store views. Performance changes are described in the notes; a still image is not a benchmark.
