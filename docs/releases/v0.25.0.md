# Halcyon Video 0.25.0

A more personal store, with regular customers who browse their favorite departments and leave a corresponding car in the lot. Choose your store year from the new poster-style carousel, then get to your next movie faster with optional Quick playback.

## In this release

- **Regular customers:** shoppers reserve browsing positions, yield at shared routes, and favor individually authored departments. Customer traffic follows the time of day; each admitted visit owns a valid parking space until departure.
- **A store that follows its era:** clerk uniforms are chosen automatically from the store year. Counter balloons appear only in 1993. The photographic surroundings begin beyond the actual modeled lot, with the sidewalk and parking details retained.
- **Startup and browsing:** a poster-style year carousel, full-screen loading artwork, larger loading copy, optional Quick playback, and restored shelf position after playback. Streaming checkout keeps the store open and accepts the printed service-row controls.
- **Simpler terminal menus:** Catalog, Browsing & Rentals, and Overhead TVs are direct categories; returning from an editor takes you back to its category.
- **Completed store details:** a brand-aware spiral gumball machine, wooden bookcases and single-faced game shelves, authored game cartons and television-season slipboxes, rental-bag rest geometry, counter accessories, display supports, facade joinery and an optional exposed ceiling with trusses and ducts.
- **Presentation:** new public-asset storefront and interior images, genuine game stock and a silent recorded fly-camera tour document the available configurations.

## Verification and remaining limits

The release candidate is checked by the application, showcase-fixture and native-security CI gates. Startup, terminal navigation, streaming checkout, customer reservations and parking lifecycle have source-bound browser evidence. Captures and touch checks use Chromium; they do not establish physical-phone frame-rate acceptance.

The five-second phone entry target and large-catalog high-quality rendering work remain open. Unfinished model variants and requirements that need device, account, reference or owner acceptance remain tracked separately. This release does not publish the separate mobile showcase or claim those launch gates are complete. The existing native companion download is not rebuilt by the web and Docker release workflows.

## Try it

[Open the browser store](https://halcyon-video.github.io/halcyon-video/) without a Halcyon account or media server. Streaming providers may require their own subscriptions and sign-in.

For your Jellyfin or Plex library, use the self-hosted app. The Docker release is `ghcr.io/halcyon-video/halcyon-video:0.25.0`.
