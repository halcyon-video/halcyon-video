# Halcyon Video v0.16.1

Custom logo changes now stay saved when a Jellyfin-connected store restarts.

Store Brand edits, including installed artwork, sign extrusion, emblem edits, reset and undo, now sync with your Jellyfin account. Applying a change that restarts the app waits for the save to finish. Successive saves run in order, and queued saves from a previous member are discarded after switching accounts.

After updating, choose your installed artwork and set the sign depth again if the previous version already reverted them.
