# Halcyon Video 0.30.3

A small fix release: the store keeps quiet while you are elsewhere, and a slow Steam can no longer hold it up.

- When the store's window is in the background, behind a game or on another desktop, it no longer reacts to your controller or plays sound, from the moment it starts.
- If Steam does not answer within 30 seconds, the store opens anyway instead of waiting, and the Steam companion frees a stuck request rather than holding it until a restart.
- In the chain-format store, a small library listed ahead of a large one no longer pushes every movie behind the counter; the movies stay on the floor facing the entrance.
- Nintendo 64 shelves carry lettered N64 plaques when RomM supplies the wrong console logo for them.
- The rental terminal's title bar shows which release you are running, and feature requests now point to the Discord forum.
