# Optional later checkout till housing

`fixtures/late-era-fixtures-2012/checkout-hardware/model.glb`, then the same
path under `public/user-assets`. The source photograph, editable Blender file,
script and provenance stay in that private collection. The public fallback is
the existing clear desktop. Earlier themes and the single-computer desk do not
load it.

The contact origin uses feet, Y up, with the fitted front facing +Z. The
existing counter-top anchor at +1.7 feet along the island supplies height and
yaw, between the membership frame and right terminal equipment. There are no
new navigation, stock or interaction targets, and no opening animation.

The photograph supports a fitted front with two narrow slots and a circular
lock-like fitting; it does not establish moving-drawer construction. Dimensions,
concealed shell construction and feet are estimates, documented with the local
source. No claim of exact manufacturer or period measurements is made.

The local mesh has 2,300 triangles, ten mesh primitives, four material roles,
UVs and normals, zero textures, and a 153,924-byte GLB. Its envelope is
1.5 × 0.35 × 1.127 feet (width, height, depth). Blender authoring and export ran
on the ThinkPad. The loader preserves the empty fallback on failure, disposes
late detached arrivals, and uses existing signage geometry/material ownership.
